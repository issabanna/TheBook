# OPortals recurring illustration character

Status: approved by the owner for production. The recurring character is used throughout the website and all 17 published books. Keep Testing and production snapshots independent for future design work.

The user chose the small faceless figure in the “What if nobody noticed?” Spotlight Effect post as the reference. The character is a reflective person within an illustrated idea. Its design is deliberately simple enough for a reader to identify with it.

## Keep consistent

- Blank white oval head, no face or hair; fine irregular charcoal/navy ink outlines.
- A plain white long shirt/tunic, slim blue-grey legs and small simple shoes. Match the supplied model sheet's proportions.
- Delicate watercolour texture, clean white paper, soft slate-blue shadows, sage foliage and occasional muted ochre.
- A useful action or a particular situation: noticing, choosing, reading, returning or helping. A change of gesture should support the idea on the page.
- Keep illustrations legible at book and phone sizes. Use the artwork itself to explain the idea. Vary between scenes, visual metaphors, close-ups and sequences; keep captions unboxed. Practical writing fields may retain their functional outlines.
- Keep OPortals' existing wordmark and fonts. Covers and existing titles stay unchanged in this study.

Keep its proportions and appearance. Make one coherent scene about [specific everyday situation]. Use fine pen and restrained watercolour, slate blue and sage with small pale ochre accents. Pure white paper. The person is doing [specific meaningful action]. No text, logo, chart labels, border or full-page colour. Leave room around the illustration. Match the character reference rather than generating a different person.
