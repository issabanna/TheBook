# Postcard-inspired interior revision — Testing only

The supplied 28-slide ZIP was treated as a visual reference, not as instructions or as authoritative philosophical source material. None of its quotations, research implications, or author attributions were imported into the books. The existing PDF editions remain the textual source.

## Implemented

- Twenty-two chapter illustrations and two additional leadership idea-page illustrations, each tied to the actual chapter/idea. The visual language follows the supplied OPortals postcards: recognisable objects, human situations, strong headings, white backgrounds, and restrained blue/olive/ochre/lavender.
- Generic pastel containers are removed in favour of plain editorial captions and marginal notes. Original diagram wording is retained. Meaningful comparisons, primary-colour graphs, reading exercises and the OBSERVED / REPORTED / INFERRED distinctions remain. Selected shading that carries meaning in Attention is intentionally preserved.
- Attention and Life Secrets use full-bleed artwork with actual translucent PDF layers behind the typeset titles. The other covers keep their distinct compositions and colours.
- All four paid samples still contain exactly four pages. Life Secrets now samples pages 1, 9, 10 and 28; Man and People pages 1, 5, 19 and 36; Lead Without the Noise pages 1, 5, 7 and 10. Attention retains pages 1, 5, 6 and 7. Website images and captions match these selections.
