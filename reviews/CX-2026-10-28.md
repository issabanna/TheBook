Scope: source/DOM and interaction review of Testing storefront and connected Social Mirror. No visual browser automation is available in this environment. No live purchase made; no conversion uplift is claimed.

## Storefront journey
- Hero has book and free-book paths; preserve that hierarchy and make primary solid/secondary outline consistent.
- Small navigation, metadata and legal text needs a readable minimum rather than repeated shrinking overrides.
- A PDF described as "Read for Free" can open an email gate. Name the action "Download free PDF"; retain ungated online pages.
- Keep exact prices, available/development status, sample links and book-specific verified checkout.
- Keep weekly content below books and link to the full journal.
- Keep the Life Secrets promotion; contain it on mobile and leave bottom content clearance.

## Social Mirror journey
- Creator does not take a quiz: use "Create my free card" instead of "Start assessment".
- Combine instruction and creation into one screen: name, five-vote explanation, optional question preview, create link.
- Preserve the requested two-second example rotation, but keep pause/reduced-motion support and no dependence on waiting for an example.
- Make the personal invitation visible, copyable and shareable through the device share sheet; keep private management link clearly secondary.
- Make open-results a primary action only once five responses arrive.
- Preserve no-login friend responses, unknown-option support, five-response privacy threshold and close/reopen/delete controls.
- Do not reset scroll/focus during periodic updates. Surface update errors rather than silently leaving stale counts.
- Paid analysis stays after free results, transparent $4 one-time pricing, verified separately; never imply it is needed to see/share the card.

## Remaining work before wider release
- Test the actual UI on narrow iOS/Android screens and in Instagram/Facebook in-app browsers.
- Complete a real PayPal service purchase/return/report test and book purchase test.
- Check real-world PNG download and native share support; fallback remains image download.
- Browser-cookie duplication prevention does not prove five different people. Do not describe it as identity verification.
- No email/account recovery: owners must retain their private management link. A secure account-recovery option would be a separate enhancement.
- Small invited samples and rule-derived archetypes are reflection tools, not validated personality measurements.
- Measure unique non-owner journeys and invitation-to-response conversion before evaluating performance. Existing aggregate counts include testing.
