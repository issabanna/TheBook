# Seventeen-title Testing bookstore

Testing now has 10 Originals and 7 Reimagined titles. The existing seven titles remain; ten new complete short illustrated books have been added at a planned $7.99 USD each. The live storefront, its covers, published PDFs, Social Mirror snapshot and existing PayPal products are not promoted or replaced.

New Originals: The Space Before Yes; Think Again, Gently; The Quiet Art of Enough; When Life Changes.

New Reimagined companions: Meditations, Reimagined (Marcus Aurelius); Epictetus: A Freer Mind; Epicurus: The Art of Enough; Plato: The Examined Life; Aristotle: A Life of Character; Seneca: On the Shortness of Life. These contain independent OPortals explanations, examples, vector illustrations and exercises, with source references and working source links. They are not translations or authorised editions.

Each new PDF has 22 pages: a distinct illustrated cover, introduction, linked reading map, eight two-page idea/application chapters, seven-day field guide, reusable reflection sheet and credits/reading references. Typography follows the approved Anton/Open Sans social-media book direction. Conceptual diagrams use compact rectangles with subtle borders. Reading pages keep the 80/20 main-text/margin-note arrangement. The ten new books total 220 pages; samples total 40 actual pages, selected from full-book positions 1, 4, 5 and 10. Existing book text and designs are unchanged.

The Testing shelf uses six columns on wide desktop screens, then five/four/three at intermediate widths and two on phones. Covers fill square frames with the crop biased toward the title. Descriptions are limited to two lines; prices, credited authors and preview actions remain visible. Filter buttons show 10 Originals and 7 Reimagined.

Samples are public on Testing. Complete PDFs require the existing native owner identity or the password-admin session. All twenty new PDF assets are encrypted with the separate runtime secret TESTING_CATALOGUE_KEY; existing edition records and their keys remain intact. New unprefixed book pages, files and cover media return 404, including for owners. There is no new public download directory. Private manuscripts and plaintext PDFs are excluded from the deployment. The new titles have no active purchase link; their planned price is shown with a clear checkout-coming-soon status. Existing book prices and payment URLs remain unchanged.
