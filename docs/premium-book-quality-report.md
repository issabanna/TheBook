## Collection design system

DejaVu Serif provides editorial titles, DejaVu Sans readable explanations and DejaVu Serif Italic quiet image notes. Actual text fonts are embedded. Olive #68734b, blue #3064bf, muted yellow #dfbb62 and lavender #afa2cb connect the collection; deep teal, olive and plum give each book a distinct cover. Warm neutral paper replaces white reading pages. Covers and chapter openings use portrait editorial artwork; explanatory diagrams remain for comparisons, sequences and reflective inventories. Roughly two-thirds of major reading/chapter visuals are conceptual scenes, with small variations by title. No ratio was imposed on source notes or workbooks.

## Editorial changes and their reasons

The core arguments were preserved, rather than rewritten to sound more commercial. New opening scenes make the ordinary difficulty concrete. Captions explain the intended metaphor without treating it as proof. The Life Secrets layout was tightened after review to avoid padding each idea across two pages. Its former introductory “secrets” qualification and original edition note remain. Source-book concepts, exercises and references were not silently removed.

The original Life Secrets currently sold on the public homepage remains unchanged. This revised edition and its sample are offered for review on Testing. Prices and payment configuration were not changed.

## Checks performed

- All 126 final pages rendered; all-page contact sheets and selected full-size pages inspected. An introductory overlap was corrected during review.
- All 66 retained reading entries checked against source bodies and questions. Life Secrets examples, experiments and original end-matter lines checked separately. Source introductions and source/edition notes retained.
- Text bounds checked on every page. Paid-book contents links checked against valid destinations. Samples omit dangling links to pages outside the sample.
- Three eight-page samples extracted from the revised books. Sampler excerpts checked against source text and rendered appearance; small glyph-antialiasing differences from PDF resource merging are tolerated, not presented as pixel-identical.
- Every sampler URL checked for the intended OPortals Testing book route. The catalog retains free access, $7.90 displayed paid prices, and purchase restrictions on unfinished titles.
- Full-book review files remain owner-protected; public samples remain public. The live homepage and its original paid-file assets are preserved.

These are digital reading editions. They contain searchable text and embedded fonts but are not certified PDF/UA or print-production PDF/X files. No independent philosophy review, accessibility certification, device reading panel or sales-value study has been completed; the requested 9/10 targets are goals, not claimed ratings. The original high-resolution illustration assets are retained separately. The artwork and layout were reviewed, but that does not guarantee that every reader will interpret a metaphor in the same way.
