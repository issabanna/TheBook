# OPortals illustrated reading edition: editorial plan

## Collection system

Philosophy for Everyday Life. One meaningful idea, one memorable illustration, one new way to see an ordinary situation. DejaVu Serif and DejaVu Sans connect the books to the website and original Life Secrets. Olive, blue, muted yellow and lavender carry meaning within warm neutral compositions. Each title has a distinct dominant palette. Human-scale scenes alternate with explanatory diagrams; diagrams are retained where comparison or sequence is the useful explanation. Text remains searchable and fonts are embedded. Illustrations are interpretive aids, never evidence for a philosophical claim.

Each paid book opens with a specific ordinary difficulty, an image of that difficulty and an invitation to examine it. Linked contents lead into chapter openings, illustrated reading pages, practical experiments and source/edition notes. Significant illustration pages are intentionally added where placing a meaningful scene beside the full existing explanation would otherwise make text too small. No filler or promises of transformation.

## The Art of Attention: reference edition

Retain the six chapters, all eighteen selected reading/practice entries, existing introduction, reflection questions, closing and references to Epictetus, Marcus Aurelius and William James. Preserve the distinction between attention and productivity, legitimate interruptions, constraints, care, rest and returning without self-accusation. Add short, explicitly fictional contemporary scenes; they illustrate the argument rather than support it empirically. Replace most abstract diagrams with doorways, overlooked human moments, prepared rooms and the experience of returning. Retain selected diagrams for control, return notes and reflection. Review chapter one before extending the layout and illustration language.

## Life Secrets

Retain six themes, eighteen ideas, every ordinary-life example, experiment, reflection question, seven-day practice, workbook and final qualification. Build a coherent visual journey with Maya (short dark curly bob, blue blouse, olive trousers) and Sam (short dark hair, olive sweater, beige trousers), fictional adults with consistent appearances. Show familiar decisions and incomplete outcomes rather than success montages. Keep the established title; clarify the subtitle as “Eighteen philosophical ideas for the life you actually have.” Revised edition is prepared for Testing only; do not overwrite the encrypted edition sold by the live homepage.

## Man and People

Retain all six chapters and thirty reading/practice entries, reader workbook, reading map, source notes and selective-commentary qualifications. Preserve distinctions between personal life, encounters, impersonal usages and evaluation. Use crowds, inherited routes, a greeting, overlapping viewpoints and social thresholds to clarify ideas without equating society with a single person. Contemporary scenes are marked OPortals applications, not Ortega's examples or quotations. Keep attribution to José Ortega y Gasset and El hombre y la gente (1957); make no authorisation or adaptation licence claim.

## SneakPeak Ideas

Rebuild the free anthology from actual revised paid-book pages, with a brief route into each book, clear source labels and direct clickable Testing book-page links. Separate complete revised titles from the four manuscripts in Closed Reader Testing. Preserve a real excerpt from each development title and its status. End with a clear invitation to explore the books. This sampler uses the same illustrations and type as the full books, not a superior visual treatment reserved for marketing.

## Review gates

1. Inspect source PDFs and reconcile manuscript text against them.
2. Build and render the first Attention chapter; review hierarchy, legibility, philosophical accuracy and image meaning.
3. Extend to complete editions; preserve source concepts, exercises and attribution.
4. Render and inspect all pages, audit text bounds and link destinations, check font embedding and download sizes.
5. Update Testing covers and samples. Keep public homepage, original paid Life Secrets file and payment configuration unchanged.

Targets are editorial goals, not independently validated scores or evidence that readers will buy or benefit. Bookmarked/searchable PDFs do not by themselves establish PDF/UA accessibility compliance. Any unavailable check or component will be identified in the delivery report.

## Implemented outcome

The finished editions contain 32, 31, 44 and 19 pages respectively. The separate-example-page Life Secrets prototype was replaced with a compact paired-column treatment to avoid padding. All eighteen examples and experiments remain. See premium-book-quality-report.md for the delivered changes and verification limits.
