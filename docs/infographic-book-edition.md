# OPortals: minimalist infographic edition

This edition replaces the painterly reading editions in Testing. The prior PDFs are retained locally under private/checkpoints/painterly-edition and the previous hosted version remains available for rollback.

## Design

Large white serif headings sit in solid blue, olive and plum title bands. Warm neutral reading pages use large information panels, connected lines, numbered steps, comparisons, branches and source-specific diagrams. Full-width arguments and practical reflection strips make the reading pages feel more complete while avoiding decorative filler. The covers use the same large type and simple connected concepts. All four PDFs use vector artwork rather than raster scenes.

Life Secrets pairs the argument and practical exercise with a compact infographic and the original Maya/Sam example. The Art of Attention distinguishes signals from consequences, shows return notes and the conditions for attention. Man and People uses different diagrams for personal encounters, social usages, inherited language and expectations. Its author attribution and qualifications are preserved.

## Scope and verification

The Art of Attention remains 32 pages; Life Secrets 31; Man and People 44; SneakPeak Ideas 19. The manuscript bodies, examples, exercises, reflection questions, introductions and source/edition notes were checked for preservation. Contents links and text bounds passed the existing checks. All pages were rendered and representative pages reviewed in detail. The free sampler uses actual updated book pages, with separately labelled Closed Reader Testing excerpts, and its 24 links were checked. Three eight-page samples and the Testing cover/sample images were regenerated to match.

Only Testing receives the revised Life Secrets PDF. The encrypted Life Secrets file currently sold by the public homepage, its public sample, payment code and homepage remain unchanged. These are searchable, font-embedded digital PDFs, without a claim of PDF/UA or PDF/X certification.
